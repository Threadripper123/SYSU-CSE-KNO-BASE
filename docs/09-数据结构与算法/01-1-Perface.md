Course purposes
•This course is about abstraction, design & implementation in solving 
problems.
•Abstraction (抽象). To analyze the requirements of problems and the logical 
structures of data.
•Design (设计). To design the physical structures of data and algorithms, to 
analyze the time complexity of algorithms.
•Implementation (实现). To implement the algorithms by using a specific 
computer language,  e.g. C/C++
